# Product Overview

ExampleApp is a comprehensive enterprise business management system built in PHP with a MySQL backend. It consolidates business operations into a single modular platform rather than requiring multiple disconnected tools.

## What It Does

- **Customer & Contact Management** — contact database with configurable and encrypted custom fields, CSV export.
- **Work Management & Ticketing** — advanced ticket system with GPS tracking, digital signatures, scheduling, and billing.
- **Project Management** — milestones, Gantt charts, budgeting, time tracking.
- **Inventory & Assets** — inventory, equipment, materials, and vendor tracking.
- **Financials** — invoicing, estimates, quotes, purchase orders, expenses, POS.
- **HR & Time Tracking** — timesheets, pay periods, approvals, overtime, certifications.
- **Safety & Compliance** — incident/injury reporting, checklists, certificates.
- **Form Builder** — dynamic forms with 40+ field types and PDF generation.
- **Dispatch & Field Ops** — real-time GPS mapping, route optimization, geofencing.
- **Reporting** — dynamic report engine with PDF/CSV/JSON export.

## Core Principles

- **Modular** — 100+ modules that can be enabled/disabled per customer.
- **Configuration-first** — new features are controlled by configuration and default to OFF. Configuration (gear icon / field config) is internal ROOK admin only, never customer-facing.
- **Role-based security** — granular permissions, subtab permissions, two-factor auth.
- **Multi-tenant** — each customer runs its own database (`{customer}_live_db`).
- **Mobile-optimized** — responsive workflows for field operations.

## Users

Business owners, operations managers, field staff, administrative staff, HR personnel, and safety officers across field service, construction, healthcare/social services, property management, manufacturing, and professional services.
