Copyright Act 1956 - PART VI - Miscellaneous and Supplementary Provisions
Section 50: Transitional provisions, and repeals
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Eliz2/4-5/74/section/50/enacted

PART VI Miscellaneous and Supplementary Provisions 50 Transitional provisions, and repeals (1) The transitional provisions contained in the Seventh Schedule to this Act shall have effect for the purposes of this Act; and the provisions of the Eighth Schedule to this Act shall have effect in accordance with those transitional provisions. (2) Subject to the said transitional provisions, the enactments specified in the Ninth Schedule to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
