How AI agents are rewriting the media buying playbook

By Priya Nair · Media Editor · 30 June 2026

Advertising agencies are beginning to experiment with autonomous AI agents that can plan, negotiate and optimise media campaigns in near real time, compressing workflows that once took teams of people several weeks. These systems analyse audience data, set bids and reallocate budgets continuously, promising both efficiency gains and a new degree of responsiveness to live market conditions.

Publishers, meanwhile, are reassessing how their content is accessed and valued. On some properties, automated readers and crawlers now account for a substantial share of traffic, raising difficult questions about how to distinguish genuine human audiences from machines, and how to be fairly compensated when AI systems consume content at scale.

The emerging consensus is that the question is no longer whether to engage with AI traffic, but how to price it fairly and transparently. Industry leaders argue that clear, machine-readable pricing could turn an awkward standoff between content owners and AI companies into a sustainable commercial relationship that benefits both sides.
