Updated guidance reshapes first-line prescribing in type 2 diabetes

By Dr Helen Marsh · Clinical Editor · 30 June 2026

Newly revised clinical guidance is reshaping how doctors approach first-line treatment for type 2 diabetes, placing far greater emphasis on protecting the heart and kidneys rather than focusing on blood glucose alone. Several medicines that were previously reserved for later stages of treatment are now recommended much earlier in the care pathway for patients at higher cardiovascular or renal risk.

Prescribers are advised to review the regimens of existing patients at their next scheduled appointment and to clearly document any shared decision-making before making a change. The guidance stresses that treatment should be tailored to the individual, weighing the benefits of each option against side effects, cost and the patient's own preferences.

Full prescribing notes, including dose adjustments for patients with reduced kidney function and guidance on combining therapies safely, are available to verified healthcare professionals. Clinicians are encouraged to revisit the recommendations periodically, as the evidence base in this area continues to evolve quickly.
